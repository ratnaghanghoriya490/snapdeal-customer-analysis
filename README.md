# 🛍️ Snapdeal Customer Purchasing Behaviour & Product Recommendation Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behaviour and product recommendation patterns using the Snapdeal customer dataset.

The dataset contains **800 customer records and 24 columns** covering customer demographics, purchase frequency, product categories, browsing behaviour, cart behaviour, reviews, recommendations, ratings and shopping satisfaction.

The analysis was performed using **Python** to understand customer behaviour and identify useful patterns for product recommendations and customer experience improvement.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behaviour
- Understand customer preferences across product categories
- Analyze browsing and product search behaviour
- Study cart completion and cart abandonment behaviour
- Analyze customer reviews and ratings
- Understand personalized recommendation behaviour
- Identify areas where the shopping experience can be improved
- Generate useful business insights from customer data

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer Behaviour Analysis

Analyzed customer demographics and purchasing frequency to understand different customer behaviour patterns.

### 2. Product Category Analysis

Analyzed the product categories purchased by customers and identified common purchasing preferences.

### 3. Browsing & Search Behaviour

Studied browsing frequency, product search methods and search result exploration behaviour.

### 4. Cart Behaviour

Analyzed:

- Add-to-cart behaviour
- Cart completion frequency
- Cart abandonment factors
- Save-for-later behaviour

This helps understand where customers may drop out during the purchase journey.

### 5. Review & Rating Analysis

Analyzed:

- Review importance
- Review reliability
- Review helpfulness
- Rating accuracy
- Review behaviour

### 6. Recommendation Analysis

Studied personalized recommendation frequency and customer perception of recommendation usefulness.

### 7. Customer Satisfaction

Analyzed shopping satisfaction and service appreciation to understand the customer experience.
